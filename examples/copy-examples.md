# Copy examples (fictional)

Replace brackets with facts from `company.md`. Do not invent metrics.

## Email — reject

```
Hi {{firstName}},

I hope this email finds you well! I love what Example Co is doing. We are a
leading platform that helps companies transform operations. Can you hop on a
30-minute call Tuesday?
```

## Email — approve (no fabricated numbers)

```
Hi {{firstName}},

Dispatchers at multi-truck HVAC shops usually lose the afternoon to after-hours
callbacks that never made it into the board.

We built Relaydesk so the next job is on a tech's phone without a group text
thread. If I sent a one-page of how that board looks, would it be useful?

Jordan
```

Why: specific role + situation, no fake "47% reduction", low-friction CTA, no link.
